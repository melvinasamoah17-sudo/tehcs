HOME CARE SERVICES — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 2026

Twitch Empire Home Care Services ("TEHCS," "we," "us," or "our") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the TEHCS mobile application (the "App") or visit tehcs.com. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register for an account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • A password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Google Firebase — we never see or store your password ourselves)

Booking Information

When you book a service, we collect:

  • The service requested
  • Preferred date and time
  • Service address
  • Any notes you provide about your care needs

Support Messages

If you use the in-app chat to contact our support/staff team, we store the messages you send and receive, along with your name and email, so our staff can respond to you.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect precise location data, device advertising identifiers, browsing history outside the App, or any payment card information (payments, if applicable, are handled outside the App directly with our team).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process, schedule, and fulfil your service bookings
  • To communicate with you about your bookings or support requests
  • To verify your email address and keep your account secure
  • To improve and maintain the App

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, under any circumstances.

3. Who Can See Your Information

Your booking and contact details are visible to authorized TEHCS staff and administrators, so they can deliver and manage your care services. Other clients cannot see your information.

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) as our backend infrastructure provider to store and secure your data. Firebase is operated by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy and security practices. We do not share your information with any other third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Data Security

We use Firebase's security infrastructure, including access-control rules that restrict who can read or write your data, to protect your information from unauthorized access. Only you and authorized TEHCS staff can access your booking and account details.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and booking information for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide our services to you. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the details below.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our App and services evolve. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
